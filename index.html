Model-Based Control and Policy Distillation for Origami Arms

Computational study · 2026

A coupled dynamic model for a robotic arm that combines motorized joints with pneumatic origami segments. Model-based control tracks 2.5× tighter than a decoupled PID baseline in simulation; distilling the MPC into a neural policy retains 93–94% settled success on the tested goals at much lower decision cost.

Behavioral Fidelity Is Not Mechanism

Research manuscript · 2026

Can interpretability improve the distillation of neural control policies into decision trees? Across 27 teachers on four tasks, matched-state augmentation improves response agreement, but ordinary sampling controls recover most of the gain. The study tests what the metric measures and when feature selection removes information needed for control.

Four Linearizations at Every Muscle Equilibrium

Computational study · 2026

Hill-type muscle models with switched activation dynamics and a force–velocity kink do not have one local linearization at equilibrium. They have four directional branches. This study derives the switches and measures how choosing the wrong branch affects prediction and closed-loop control.

When a Reasoning-Faithfulness Metric Meets Hard Math

Research study · September 2026

Transferring a reversed-comparison test to hard mathematics initially looks like evidence of unfaithful reasoning. Whitespace and rewording controls reveal a different explanation: the model’s stated intermediate answers are sensitive to small prompt changes, without clear evidence of direction-driven rationalization.

Optical Recruitment Control for Biohybrid Muscle

Computational study · August 2026

Can patterned light act as a synthetic motor pool for engineered muscle? A calibrated simulation finds a wider range of smooth, low force under spatial stimulation, while refuting the original endurance claim. Optical transport bounds how many tissue sectors can be addressed independently.

Ape-X MPI: Hybrid MPI/OpenACC Distributed Prioritized Experience Replay

ID5130 Parallel Scientific Computing · Course Project · with Govind S Ashan

A re-implementation of Ape-X (Horgan et al., 2018) that replaces the reference ZeroMQ/asyncio/multiprocessing transport, proxies, and queues with a single MPI_COMM_WORLD where every logical role is a fixed rank, launched with one mpirun command. The replay buffer's segment tree is ported to a C library with optional OpenACC GPU offload, giving roughly two orders of magnitude speedup, and the MPI port beats the ZMQ baseline on actor intake throughput and reward growth at every actor count tested.

Flat-Foldable Origami Tessellations as Native Continuous Toolpaths for FDM

M.Tech Thesis · IIT Madras · Advisor: Prof. Pulkit Verma · Ongoing

FDM print quality improves when a layer is deposited as one continuous, crossing-free stroke. Flat-foldable origami tessellations already have the structure this requires: by Maekawa's theorem every interior crease vertex has even degree, so the crease graph is natively Eulerian, and a printable toolpath falls out for free, with no mesh-refinement overhead. Includes a browser-based slicer, a FOLD-format extension for storing toolpaths, and a from-scratch GPU origami simulator.

origami_sim: A GPU Origami Physics Simulator, From Scratch

Skill-Building Project · Supports the FDM thesis

A from-scratch reimplementation of Ghassaei, Demaine & Gershenfeld's GPU origami simulation method (the engine behind Origami Simulator): a crease pattern becomes a mass-spring mesh driven by four validated force types and an explicit physics loop, with a NumPy reference cross-checked to round-off against a Taichi GPU port that reproduces the paper's ~78× speedup at scale.
